# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

HawkBucks is a community project. We expect all contributors and participants to help maintain a respectful, constructive, and inclusive environment.

Positive behavior includes:

- Being respectful and professional
- Giving constructive technical feedback
- Accepting different technical approaches
- Focusing criticism on code and ideas rather than people
- Helping newcomers understand the project

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or hateful conduct
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or contributions
- Publishing private or sensitive information
- Malicious behavior toward project users or contributors

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project participation.

## Reporting

For security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

For general conduct concerns, contact the project maintainer through the contact information available on the repository profile.
